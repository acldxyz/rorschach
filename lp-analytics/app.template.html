Track Record Analyzer

Load a GP's fund cash flows and portfolio operating data to build net and gross track-record charts.

Data

File formats

Headers are case-insensitive. Amounts in millions. Contributions negative, distributions positive, NAV is the reported value at a date (the latest NAV per fund is used).

cash_flows:  fund, vintage, fund_size, date, type (Contribution|Distribution|NAV), amount
investments: company, fund, sector, country, deal_type, entry_date, exit_date,
             invested, realized, unrealized,
             revenue_entry, revenue_exit, ebitda_entry, ebitda_exit,
             tev_entry, tev_exit, net_debt_entry, net_debt_exit, ownership_entry,
             founded, pre_investment, milestones ("2019-06: text | 2021-03: text"), deal_lead
team:        name, title, joined, focus, prior, education, boards
benchmarks:  vintage, metric (TVPI|IRR|DPI), top, median, bottom
